About this data

Urban Loom is a synthetic demo shop: two years of sales, customers and stock generated by scripts/generate_shop_data.py and loaded into BigQuery. A real shop points GoNoGo at its own tables (set GONOGO_DATASET), for example a nightly export from its point-of-sale, Shopify or marketplace reports; the agents and rules stay the same.
